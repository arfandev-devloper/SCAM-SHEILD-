# ScamShield Usage Guide

1. Open the app and choose **Text**, **Email**, or **Link**.
2. Paste suspicious content into the analyzer.
3. Select **Analyze Now**.
4. Review the 0–100 score, risk level and detected signals.
5. Follow the displayed safety recommendations.
6. Use **History** to review previous local scans.
7. Use **Tools** for URL checking and basic email-header analysis.

## Important
Do not paste passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or other secrets into demo projects. A low score does not prove content is safe.
